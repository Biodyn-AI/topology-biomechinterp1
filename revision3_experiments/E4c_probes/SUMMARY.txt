E4c_probes - supervised edge probes (SENSITIVITY analysis; answers R1.6 / R1.2; no pass/fail)
SUMMARY rewritten by the independent verifier (2026-10-26). The implementer's text is kept verbatim in
verify/SUMMARY_implementer_before_verification.txt. Corrections to it are listed in section V.
CONVENTION3 sha256 3e3249d1d0a8ce1bc3cf64639fe233b016535456f82432a8bbfcacdd64a05c01 (= FROZEN.txt; recorded in results.json
and verify/verification.json)

QUESTION
Can a supervised probe, given scGPT residual-stream gene embeddings of a TF and a candidate target, recover a TRRUST edge on
genes it has never seen? We tested linear and non-linear pair encodings. We then asked whether any success goes beyond
(a) what target identity alone predicts and (b) what the same probe learns from degree-preserving rewired labels.

WHAT WAS RUN
All numbers below come from probe_results.csv, probe_folds.csv, relational_signal.csv, null_results.csv, null_folds.csv,
direction_test.csv, tuned_vs_fixed.csv and rewire_stats.csv (implementer), or from verify/v*.csv (verifier).
- Pairs: cache/<ctx>/h3_pairs.npz (the same pairs as H3).
  - lung: 52,426 pairs, 5,266 positives.
  - lung_external: 50,947 / 5,093.
  - kidney_B: 36,380 / 3,668.
  - immune: node-only sanity check only.
- Embedding: each layer's V x 512 matrix of observed genes, mean-centred over V.
- Pair features:
  - concat [x_i, x_j];
  - Hadamard x_i*x_j;
  - |x_i - x_j|;
  - bilinear vec(p_i p_j^T), where p is a PCA-16 projection fitted on training-fold genes only.
  - Sensitivity combinations beyond the specification, on identical folds: [x_j, Hadamard] and [x_j, bilinear].
- Models:
  - M1: L2 logistic regression, all 12 layers. alpha is chosen from {1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1} on a regime-matched inner split of
    each training fold.
  - M2: HistGradientBoosting, on L0, L5 and L11.
  - M3: sklearn MLP with 2 hidden layers and early stopping, on L0, L5 and L11.
- Regimes:
  - C3, pair-disjoint (primary): genes are dealt into 5 folds; training pairs have both genes in training folds; test pairs have
    both genes in the held-out fold. Each C3 test fold therefore holds about 4% of pairs (lung: about 2,100 pairs, 22 TFs and
    175 positives; kidney_B: about 1,450 pairs, 15 TFs and 111 positives).
  - C2: held-out TFs.
  - C1: random pairs.
- Metric: within-TF stratified AUROC (stats3.StratifiedPairs) on every test fold. Every lung, lung_external and kidney_B fold
  qualified. In immune C3, 1 of 25 folds per layer fell back to pooled AUROC (24 fold-rows in total).
- Controls on identical folds:
  - TF-only and target-only;
  - co-expression pair features (|r|, r, mean and detection of both genes, from expr_extraction.npy);
  - ESM2-15B (UCE per-protein), 5,120-d, PCA-128 fitted per training fold. The specification calls it ESM2-3B, which is wrong.
  - the scGPT static gene-token embedding: whole-human checkpoint, encoder embedding after the encoder LayerNorm,
    subproject_53 npz, 512-d, centred over V;
  - the unsupervised H3 scorer (signed cosine, full 512-d) and |co-expression|, scored on the same test folds.
- Null: degree-preserving MCMC target swaps restricted to the h3 candidate universe. Every TF's and every target's positive count
  is preserved exactly (checked for all 100 draws by both verifiers). 100 draws for M1 and 10 for M2/M3, each retrained under
  the same CV.
  - A rewired draw retains 20-23% of the true positives. An independent 50x-burn-in chain gives the same retention, so this is
    the stationary overlap of the constrained space, not poor mixing.
  - Positive retention makes the null conservative for relational signal. The null is NOT clean for additive (target-level)
    signal: see Direct evidence 3c.
- Intervals:
  - Implementer: Nadeau-Bengio (NB) corrected resampled t over outer folds, var = (1/J + n_test/n_train) s^2, with the ratio
    counted in pairs (C3: about 0.0625).
  - Verifier sensitivity: the same with ratio 0.25. In C3 the held-out unit is a fifth of the genes, and 0.25 is the ordinary
    5-fold value.
  - Both are reported below as [pair-ratio CI | gene-ratio CI].
  - Empirical p = (b+1)/(N+1). Max-T over the 12 layers uses shared rewiring draws (stats3.max_t_adjust).
    stats3.assert_resolution was applied to every null test.
- CONVENTION3_ERRATA item 3 (vertex-subsampling delete-d scale) does not apply. E4c computes no network-statistic interval and
  uses no vertex subsampling.

REDUCTIONS (compute; listed in results.json)
- C1 has 1 repeat. C2 and C3 have 5 x 5 for M1.
- The M1 100-draw null was run on C3 repeat 0 only (5 folds).
- M2/M3 were run only on C3 and C2 repeat 0, with concat and Hadamard features and with scGPT and co-expression sources only.
  - Their TF-only control is 0.5 by construction under the within-TF metric.
  - Their null has 10 draws (resolution floor 0.091), so no significance claim is possible. The M2 null covers concat only.
  - M2/M3 hyperparameters were fixed a priori, with only early stopping in-fold. A tuned variant (small grids, regime-matched
    inner split) was added for true labels only.
- Run-to-run SD is the SD over the 5 CV repetitions (M1 only).

DIRECT EVIDENCE
1. Unseen genes (C3) give low AUROCs.
   - Best C3 probe means: 0.597 (lung, scGPT bilinear, L2); 0.591 (lung_external, bilinear, L8); 0.589 (kidney_B,
     [x_j, bilinear], L0).
   - Upper NB bounds of the best scGPT probes: 0.621-0.625 with the pair ratio and 0.638-0.647 with the gene ratio. The 0.60 level
     is not excluded for them.
   - Other sources in lung reach 0.581 (static token), 0.567 (ESM2-15B) and 0.512 (co-expression).
   - SD across CV repetitions is <= 0.027 AUROC in the three main contexts (M1, C3). Immune target-only reaches 0.070.
2. Target identity carries most of what is learnable.
   - Target-only probes reach 0.540-0.573 in C3, 0.600-0.632 in C2 and 0.600-0.631 in C1. TF-only is exactly 0.500 within TF.
   - Linear concat probes are additive, f(x_i) + g(x_j), so within a TF they reduce to target-only.
     - Their excess over target-only is -0.004 to +0.004 in C3.
     - The excess is a consistent +0.002 to +0.005 in C2/C1 (pair-ratio interval > 0 at 5-10 of 12 layers). This is expected,
       not relational: the TF block acts as TF fixed effects in the pooled loss, which improves the target weights.
   - Hadamard and |diff| probes are never above target-only in any context or regime (no interval > 0).
     - For |diff| in C3 the interval is below 0 at 7/12 (lung), 12/12 (lung_external) and 9/12 (kidney_B) layers with the pair
       ratio, and at 0/12, 8/12 and 5/12 with the gene ratio.
3. Interaction (bilinear) signal is small and layer-dependent, and it does not reach kidney_B.
   a. Bilinear minus target-only, C3, 25 folds:
      - lung: +0.015 to +0.051 at L0-L9, falling to -0.004 at L11. The interval is > 0 at 7/12 layers with the pair ratio and at
        1/12 with the gene ratio. L2: +0.051 [0.026, 0.076 | 0.009, 0.093].
      - lung_external: +0.002 to +0.029. The interval is > 0 at 2/12 layers (L8, L9) with the pair ratio and at 0/12 with the
        gene ratio.
      - kidney_B: -0.031 to +0.006, no layer > 0.
   b. Against the same probe trained on rewired labels (C3 repeat 0, N = 100):
      - lung: p = 0.0099 (the floor) at 11/12 layers and p = 0.030 at L11. Max-T < 0.05 at 11/12.
      - lung_external: p < 0.05 at 8/12, max-T < 0.05 at 4/12 (L6-L9).
      - kidney_B: p < 0.05 at 4/12, max-T < 0.05 at 1/12.
   c. Added by the verifier (verify/v1_rewired_did.csv): the rewired null is not clean for additive signal. The additive concat
      probe, which carries no within-TF relational information, still differs from its rewired-label version.
      - It is above it in lung (raw p < 0.05 at 9/12 layers, max-T at 1/12).
      - It is below it in lung_external (true 0.548-0.563 vs rewired 0.562-0.574).
      - So 3b mixes an additive shift with interaction signal.
      - A difference-in-differences test isolates the interaction term: D = AUROC(bilinear) - AUROC(concat) on true labels,
        against the same D on each rewired draw. Rewired-label D is negative everywhere (-0.008 to -0.036), because a bilinear form
        cannot use the target-level structure that rewiring preserves.
        - lung: D = +0.026 to +0.046 at L0-L7. Max-T < 0.05 at 7/12 layers (L1-L7).
        - lung_external: D = +0.000 to +0.028. Max-T < 0.05 at 7/12 layers (L4-L10; three of them at exactly 0.0495).
        - kidney_B: 0/12.
   d. Combined relational signal (implementer) = probe minus max(rewired-label AUROC, target-only), C3 repeat 0 (J = 5):
      - lung: the interval excludes 0 at 1 layer with the pair ratio (L6: +0.038 [0.004, 0.072]) and at 0 layers with the gene
        ratio (L6: [-0.006, 0.083]).
      - lung_external and kidney_B: 0 layers.
   e. Adding a bilinear block to the target-only block ([x_j, bilinear]) gains:
      - lung: +0.001 to +0.025 (interval > 0 at 8/12 layers with the pair ratio, 3/12 with the gene ratio);
      - lung_external: +0.009 to +0.022 (9/12 | 3/12);
      - kidney_B: <= +0.016 (0/12 | 0/12).
4. The interaction signal is not specific to the contextual residual stream.
   - Static token embedding, lung: bilinear minus target-only +0.026 [0.003, 0.050 | -0.013, 0.066]. It beats its rewired-label
     version at p = 0.0099.
   - ESM2-15B, lung: +0.005 [-0.021, 0.031], with rewired p = 0.0099.
   - Paired same-fold comparisons (verify/v3_paired_sources.csv):
     - scGPT bilinear minus static bilinear has no layer with an interval > 0 in any context. It is < 0 at 4/12 layers in lung
       and at 5/12 in kidney_B.
     - The scGPT excess over its own target-only exceeds the static excess at 1/12 layers in lung (L2: +0.025 [0.003, 0.047],
       pair ratio). It exceeds the ESM2-15B excess at 1/12 in lung and at 0/12 elsewhere.
   - DiD (verify/v8_did_sources.csv) is also positive against rewiring for the layer-free sources:
     - ESM2-15B: p = 0.0495 (lung), 0.0198 (lung_external), 0.079 (kidney_B).
     - static: p = 0.069, 0.099, 0.21.
5. Against the unsupervised scorers on identical C3 folds:
   - The cosine scorer itself gives 0.521-0.561 on these folds.
   - scGPT bilinear minus the signed-cosine H3 scorer: +0.020 to +0.059 in lung (interval > 0 at 10/12 layers with the pair
     ratio, 3/12 with the gene ratio) and +0.035 to +0.062 in lung_external (12/12 | 0/12). kidney_B: -0.009 to +0.016 (0/12).
   - scGPT bilinear minus |co-expression|: interval > 0 at 12/12 layers in both lungs with the pair ratio (8/12 and 5/12 with the
     gene ratio).
6. Non-linear probes add nothing detectable on unseen genes.
   - M2/M3 C3 AUROCs are 0.49-0.56.
   - In C3, no M2/M3 probe, fixed or tuned, exceeds its own target-only control with an interval above 0. Several are below it,
     e.g. lung_external M3 Hadamard L5, tuned: -0.065 [-0.097, -0.032].
   - In C2 (held-out TFs, targets seen), 5 of 72 scGPT M2/M3 probe rows exceed target-only with an interval above 0. All 5 are
     concat rows (5 of 36 concat rows), by +0.028 to +0.041: lung M2 L11, lung_external M2/M3/M3-tuned L11, kidney_B M2-tuned L0. These are single repetitions, with no
     multiplicity adjustment and no null.
   - Tuning inside folds changes AUROC by -0.043 to +0.039 over both regimes. Mean change: +0.007 (M2) and -0.002 (M3) in C3;
     +0.010 and +0.002 in C2.
   - Against 10 rewired draws, lung M3 has z = 4.6 (concat L0) and z = 2.6-3.7 (Hadamard). These carry no significance claim,
     because N = 10 is below the resolution needed.
7. Ordered direction test (M1 concat, C3, both genes held out). Pairs are TRRUST i->j with both genes Lambert TFs and j->i
   undocumented. The fraction of pairs scored forward > reverse:
   - lung, 435 pairs over 241 genes: 0.494-0.543. No layer's interval excludes 0.5.
   - lung_external, 453 pairs over 251 genes: 0.536-0.594.
   - kidney_B, 325 pairs over 197 genes: 0.563-0.628.
   - The implementer's exact binomial CIs treat pairs as independent. They exclude 0.5 at 11/12 (lung_external) and 12/12
     (kidney_B) layers.
   - The pairs share genes, so the verifier used a gene-cluster bootstrap (B = 2,000; pair weight = product of gene weights;
     verify/v6_direction_cluster.csv). With it, the interval excludes 0.5 at only 2/12 layers in lung_external (L10, L11; L11:
     0.594 [0.506, 0.677]), 1/12 in kidney_B (L7: 0.628 [0.512, 0.740]) and 0/12 in lung.
   - An independent sklearn refit of lung_external L11 with the verifier's own alpha selection gives 0.570 instead of 0.594 (92%
     sign agreement per pair), so the fraction also depends on regularisation.
   - The strict subset (j->i absent from every regulatory set) behaves the same.
   - Node-level baselines on the same pairs give 0.53-0.56 (TRRUST out-degree of i > j) and 0.55-0.59 (in-degree of j > i).
8. Immune sanity check.
   - TF-only is 0.498-0.501 (exactly 0.500 where no fold is pooled).
   - Target-only is 0.61-0.62 in C1 and 0.57-0.58 in C2.
   - In C3 target-only is 0.47-0.50. Immune C3 folds hold about 3 TFs and 18 positives, so they are uninformative.

INFERENCE
- On held-out genes, a small amount of TF-specific target preference is learnable from pairs of scGPT gene embeddings.
  - It appears only through an explicit low-rank interaction (bilinear) term.
  - It is about +0.02 to +0.05 AUROC above a target-only model in lung at L0-L7, at an absolute AUROC below 0.60.
  - Its interval against target-only is robust to the interval choice at L2 only. It is smaller and interval-fragile in
    lung_external, and absent in kidney_B.
- The rank-based tests do not depend on the NB ratio:
  - Interaction beyond an additive model, relative to degree-preserving rewiring (DiD), is detected in both lungs at 7 of 12
    layers each.
  - It is not detected in kidney_B.
  - The lung -> lung_external sign agreement holds on this statistic. The DiD is a verifier-added sensitivity test, not a
    pre-registered criterion.
- The same structure is present in context-free gene representations: the static token embedding and, by DiD, ESM2-15B.
  - The likeliest reading is a property of gene identity, i.e. which genes tend to be co-regulated or co-annotated. It is not a
    property of the cell-contextual representation.
- Boosting and MLPs over concatenated or Hadamard features find nothing more on unseen genes. Within this probe family and data
  size, we found no evidence of a stronger non-linear or distributed encoding.
- For a linear concat probe, the forward-minus-reverse score is a node-score difference, (w_TF/s_TF - w_tg/s_tg).(x_i - x_j),
  up to block standardisation.
  - The direction test therefore measures a node-level regulator-versus-target propensity, not a relation.
  - Once gene sharing is accounted for, it is weak (2/12 layers in lung_external, 1/12 in kidney_B, 0/12 in lung). It does not
    meet the replication pattern: null in lung.

HYPOTHESIS (not tested here)
- The bilinear term may pick up shared functional-module or annotation structure among TRRUST literature edges (study bias), not
  mechanistic regulation. The parallels in the static embedding and ESM2-15B fit this explanation. We have not tested it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
- It does not show that scGPT encodes no regulatory relations. It shows that these probe families recover at most a small
  relational component on unseen genes: linear, bilinear PCA-16, HGB and a 2-layer MLP, trained on about 20-35k TRRUST-benchmark
  pairs.
- It does not show direction. The direction result is node-level and weak under a gene-cluster interval.
- It makes no pass/fail claim and no H3 decision. This is a sensitivity analysis.

LIMITATIONS AND FAILURE MODES
- C3 test folds are small (see WHAT WAS RUN), and fold AUROCs are noisy (fold SD median 0.037, up to 0.056).
- Each C3 repetition evaluates only the about 20% of pairs whose two genes fall in the same fifth.
- Interval method matters.
  - The NB correction assumes exchangeable folds. The pair-count ratio (0.0625) gives intervals 1.68x narrower than the
    gene-level ratio (0.25) for J = 25, and 1.31x narrower for J = 5.
  - Every "interval > 0 at n/12 layers" count above is given under both ratios. The gene-ratio counts are the cautious reading.
- The rewired null covers C3 repeat 0 only.
  - It retains about 21% of true positives, which makes it conservative for relational signal.
  - It is not neutral for additive target-level signal (3c).
  - Max-T covers the 12 layers but not the features or sources. Multiplicity across feature families is not adjusted beyond the
    BH column in relational_signal.csv.
- M2/M3 cover a reduced grid. Their 10-draw nulls cannot support significance claims, and the tuned nulls were not run.
- Labels are TRRUST. "Candidate non-edges" are undocumented, not confirmed absent.

SURPRISES
- The static, context-free token embedding matches the residual-stream bilinear signal in lung. ESM2-15B passes the DiD test in
  lung_external.
- The additive concat probe beats rewired labels in lung but falls below them in lung_external, so a rewired-label null is not
  a pure relation null for supervised probes.
- Fitting the true-label column jointly with the 100 rewired columns can change the in-fold alpha choice.
  - 3 of 171 M1 null rows differ from the stand-alone true-label run by more than 0.01 on one fold: kidney_B concat L5 0.063, L8
    0.059; lung concat L6 0.012. The median difference is 8e-5 (null_results.csv, max_abs_diff_vs_true_run).
  - Null comparisons use the jointly fitted column, so they stay internally consistent.
- The target-only control is far from 0.5 (0.54-0.63), even though non-edges are matched on target in-degree and detection
  deciles.

Checks passed:
(1) Design and status.
    - E4c is a sensitivity analysis (CONVENTION3 §5, E4 extras). It runs no H1-H4 statistic, so §4 decision rules do not apply.
    - Pairs, metric (stats3.StratifiedPairs) and the empirical-p rule match CONVENTION3.
(2) Embedding.
    - Only observed genes are used (Ctx.layer asserts no zero rows; v1: observed rows non-zero, unobserved rows zero).
    - Each layer is V-centred.
    - No SVD basis is indexed. The PCA-16 / PCA-128 bases are top right-singular vectors of training-gene rows, re-centred on
      those genes.
(3) Gene alignment (v1_alignment.csv).
    - obs_genes equals the h5ad var index at obs_idx in all three contexts.
    - The h3 labels equal TRRUST membership.
    - Every candidate non-edge is absent from every regulatory set in both directions.
    - Pair counts match.
(4) Leakage.
    - PCA, standardisation and alpha selection are inside training folds. Inner splits are regime-matched.
    - The C3 train/test gene overlap is 0 and the C2 TF overlap is 0, over all repeats (v1_folds.csv).
    - Node-only baselines, reference scorers and nulls use identical deterministic folds.
    - Leaks found: none. Only label-free quantities use all of V: centring, the static/co-expression features, and the PCA fitted
      on all training genes, which include the inner-validation genes and is used only for alpha choice.
(5) Nulls.
    - All 100 draws preserve every TF's and every target's positive count (v1).
    - The swap proposal is symmetric, so its stationary law is uniform on the constrained space.
    - The empirical p and max-T values were recomputed from raw per-draw checkpoints: max |diff| 1e-16.
(6) Resolution. At N = 100 the floor is 0.0099, so claims at 0.05 are supported. The M2/M3 N = 10 nulls carry no claims (correct).
(7) Bootstrap unit and errata. No vertex subsampling, so errata item 3 does not apply. Edge intervals are NB (allowed by the
    specification). The verifier added gene-level NB and a gene-cluster bootstrap where the unit matters.
(8) Model labels.
    - ESM2-15B (UCE per-protein, 5,120-d) is correct everywhere.
    - The static source is the scGPT whole-human checkpoint gene-token embedding after the encoder LayerNorm
      (subproject_53_scgpt_gpl_replication/scripts/phase0_extract_embeddings.py).
(9) SUMMARY numbers were checked line by line against the CSVs (verify/v4_summary_check.py/.log).
