E4c_probes - supervised edge probes (SENSITIVITY analysis; answers R1.6 / R1.2; no pass/fail)
CONVENTION3 sha256 3e3249d1d0a8ce1bc3cf64639fe233b016535456f82432a8bbfcacdd64a05c01 (recorded in results.json)

QUESTION
Can a supervised probe, given scGPT residual-stream gene embeddings of a TF and a candidate target,
recover a TRRUST edge on genes it has never seen? We tested linear and non-linear pair encodings, and
asked whether any success goes beyond (a) what target identity alone predicts and (b) what a probe can
learn from degree-preserving rewired labels.

WHAT WAS RUN (all numbers below come from probe_results.csv, relational_signal.csv, null_results.csv,
direction_test.csv, tuned_vs_fixed.csv, rewire_stats.csv and results.json in this directory)
- Pairs: cache/<ctx>/h3_pairs.npz. lung: 52,426 pairs, 5,266 positives. lung_external: 50,947 / 5,093.
  kidney_B: 36,380 / 3,668. immune was used only for the node-only sanity check.
- Embedding: each layer's V x 512 matrix, V-centred, observed genes only.
- Pair features: concat [x_i, x_j], Hadamard, |x_i - x_j|, bilinear vec(p_i p_j^T) with PCA-16 fitted on
  training-fold genes. Sensitivity combinations (beyond the specification) on identical folds:
  [x_j, Hadamard] and [x_j, bilinear].
- Models: M1 L2 logistic regression (12 layers; alpha tuned on a regime-matched inner split);
  M2 HistGradientBoosting and M3 MLP (2 hidden layers, early stopping) on L0, L5 and L11.
- Regimes: C3 pair-disjoint (the primary regime: TF and target both held-out genes), C2 held-out TFs,
  C1 random pairs.
- Metric: within-TF stratified AUROC (stats3.StratifiedPairs) on every test fold. Every lung,
  lung_external and kidney_B fold qualified; 24 immune C3 folds fell back to pooled AUROC.
- Controls on identical folds: TF-only, target-only, co-expression pair features, ESM2-15B (UCE
  per-protein, 5,120-d, PCA-128 per training fold; the specification calls it ESM2-3B, which is wrong),
  and the scGPT static token embedding. Added: the unsupervised H3 primary scorer (signed cosine, full
  512-d) and |co-expression| scored on the same test folds.
- Null: degree-preserving MCMC target swaps restricted to the candidate universe. Every TF's and target's
  positive count was preserved exactly. 100 draws for M1 and 10 for M2/M3, each retrained under the same
  CV. A rewired draw retains 20-23% of true positives, against 10.8% expected for uniform within-TF
  redraws (rewire_stats.csv), so the null is somewhat conservative.
- Intervals: Nadeau-Bengio corrected resampled t over outer folds (95%). Empirical p = (b+1)/(N+1).
  Max-T across the 12 layers uses shared rewiring draws. stats3.assert_resolution was applied to every
  null test.
- CONVENTION3_ERRATA item 3 (vertex-subsampling scale) does not apply: E4c computes no network-statistic
  interval.

REDUCTIONS (compute; listed in results.json)
- C1 has 1 repeat; C2 and C3 have 5 x 5 for M1.
- The M1 100-draw null was run on C3 repeat 0 only (5 folds).
- M2/M3 were run only on: C3 and C2 repeat 0; concat and Hadamard features; scGPT and co-expression
  sources. Their TF-only control was not fitted, because it is 0.5 by construction under the within-TF
  metric.
- M2/M3 null: 10 draws, so the resolution floor is 0.091 and no significance claim is possible. The M2
  null covers concat only.
- M2/M3 hyperparameters were fixed a priori; only early stopping was in-fold. A tuned variant (small grids
  inside training folds) was added on the same folds for true labels only.
- Run-to-run SD is the SD over the 5 CV repetitions (M1 only).

DIRECT EVIDENCE
1. Unseen genes (C3) give low AUROCs. The best C3 probe means are 0.597 (lung, scGPT bilinear, L2),
   0.591 (lung_external, bilinear L8) and 0.589 (kidney_B, [x_j, bilinear] L0). Upper NB bounds reach
   0.621-0.625 for the best scGPT probes, so the 0.60 level is not excluded for them. The other sources
   in lung reach 0.581 (static token), 0.567 (ESM2-15B) and 0.512 (co-expression). The SD across CV
   repetitions is at most 0.027 AUROC (M1, C3).
2. Target identity carries most of what is learnable. Target-only probes reach 0.540-0.573 in C3,
   0.60-0.63 in C2 and 0.60-0.63 in C1. TF-only is exactly 0.500 within TF.
   - Linear concat probes are additive, f(x_i) + g(x_j), so within TF they reduce to target-only. Their
     excess over target-only is only -0.003 to +0.004 in C3 in all contexts.
   - Hadamard and |diff| probes are never above target-only in any context or regime (no interval > 0). For |diff| the
     interval is below 0 at 7-12 of 12 layers in C3.
3. Interaction (bilinear) signal is small, layer-dependent and does not reach kidney.
   - lung C3: bilinear exceeds target-only by +0.015 to +0.051 at L0-L9, with the interval > 0 at 7/12
     layers (L2: +0.051 [0.026, 0.076]).
   - It beats the same probe trained on rewired labels at all 12 layers (p = 0.0099, the floor at N = 100;
     max-T over layers < 0.05 at 11/12).
   - lung_external: +0.002 to +0.029 over target-only (interval > 0 at L8 and L9 only). Beats rewired
     p < 0.05 at 8/12 layers, max-T < 0.05 at 4/12 (L6-L9).
   - kidney_B: -0.031 to +0.006 (no layer > 0). Max-T < 0.05 at 1/12.
   - The combined relational signal is probe minus max(rewired-label AUROC, target-only), C3 repeat 0. Its
     interval excludes 0 at 1 layer in lung (L6: +0.038 [0.004, 0.072]) and at 0 layers in
     lung_external and kidney_B.
   - Adding a bilinear block to the target-only block ([x_j, bilinear]) gains +0.017 to +0.025 at L0-L7
     in lung (interval > 0 at 8/12) and +0.009 to +0.022 in lung_external (9/12). The gain in kidney_B is
     <= +0.016 (0/12).
4. The interaction signal is not specific to the contextual residual stream. The scGPT static token
   embedding, which has no cell context, shows a similar lung bilinear excess: +0.026 [0.003, 0.050] over
   target-only, and it beats rewired labels at p = 0.0099. ESM2-15B bilinear: +0.005 [-0.021, 0.031],
   with rewired p = 0.0099.
5. Against the unsupervised scorers on identical C3 folds:
   - scGPT bilinear probes exceed the signed-cosine H3 scorer by +0.020 to +0.059 in lung (interval > 0
     at 10/12 layers) and +0.035 to +0.062 in lung_external (12/12). They exceed |co-expression| at
     12/12 layers.
   - In kidney_B the gain over cosine is -0.009 to +0.016 (0/12).
   - The cosine scorer itself gives 0.521-0.561 on these folds.
6. Non-linear probes add nothing detectable.
   - M2/M3 C3 AUROCs are 0.49-0.57. No M2/M3 probe, fixed or tuned, exceeds its own target-only control
     with an interval above 0 in C3. Several are below it (e.g. lung_external M3 Hadamard L5, tuned:
     -0.065 [-0.097, -0.032]).
   - Tuning inside folds changes AUROC by -0.043 to +0.039 (mean +0.007 for M2, -0.002 for M3).
   - Against 10 rewired draws, lung M3 has z = 4.6 (concat L0) and z = 2.6-3.7 (Hadamard). These carry
     no significance claim, because N = 10 is below the resolution needed.
7. Ordered direction test (M1 concat, C3, both genes held out; TRRUST i->j with both genes Lambert TFs
   and j->i undocumented). Each row gives the fraction of pairs scored forward > reverse.
   - lung, 435 pairs: 0.494-0.543. No layer's binomial CI excludes 0.5.
   - lung_external, 453 pairs: 0.536-0.594 (CI > 0.5 at 11/12 layers).
   - kidney_B, 325 pairs: 0.563-0.628 (12/12).
   - The strict subset (j->i absent from every regulatory set) behaves the same.
   - Node-level baselines on the same pairs give 0.53-0.56 (TRRUST out-degree of i > j) and 0.55-0.59
     (in-degree of j > i).
8. Immune sanity check. TF-only is 0.500 (0.498-0.502 in C3, where one fold is pooled). Target-only is
   0.61-0.62 in C1 and 0.57-0.58 in C2. In C3 it is 0.47-0.50, but immune C3 folds hold about 3 TFs and
   18 positives, so they are uninformative.

INFERENCE
- On held-out genes, a small amount of TF-specific target preference is learnable from pairs of scGPT
  gene embeddings. It shows up only through an explicit low-rank interaction (bilinear) term. It is
  detectable against degree-preserving rewired labels in lung and, more weakly, in lung_external.
- Its size is about +0.02 to +0.05 AUROC above a target-only model, at an absolute AUROC below 0.60. It
  is absent in kidney_B. Adding it to the target-only block gives about +0.02.
- The same structure is present in the static token embedding. The likeliest reading is a property of
  gene identity (which genes tend to be co-regulated or co-annotated), not of the cell-contextual
  representation.
- Boosting and MLPs over concatenated or Hadamard features do not find more. Within this probe family
  and data size, we found no evidence of a stronger non-linear or distributed encoding.
- For a linear concat probe, the forward-minus-reverse score is a node-score difference,
  (w_TF - w_target)·(x_i - x_j). The direction test therefore measures a node-level
  regulator-versus-target propensity, not a relation. It fails the replication criterion: null in lung,
  positive in lung_external.

HYPOTHESIS (not tested here)
- The bilinear term may pick up shared functional-module or annotation structure among TRRUST
  literature edges (study bias), not mechanistic regulation. The static-embedding parallel fits that
  explanation. We have not tested it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
- It does not show that scGPT encodes no regulatory relations. It shows that these probe families
  (linear, bilinear PCA-16, HGB, 2-layer MLP), trained on about 20-35k TRRUST-benchmark pairs, recover
  at most a small relational component on unseen genes.
- It does not show direction: the direction result is node-level and does not replicate.
- No pass/fail and no H3 decision. This is a sensitivity analysis.

LIMITATIONS AND FAILURE MODES
- C3 test folds are small: about 22 TFs and 175 positives per fold in lung, about 15 TFs and 111
  positives in kidney_B. Fold AUROCs are noisy.
- The NB correction assumes exchangeable folds. Repetitions share genes, so the intervals may be
  optimistic. The 0.60 upper bounds should be read with that in mind.
- The rewired null covers C3 repeat 0 only. It retains about 21% of true positives, which makes it
  conservative. Max-T covers the 12 layers but not the features or sources; the multiplicity across
  feature families is not adjusted beyond the BH column in relational_signal.csv.
- M2/M3 cover a reduced grid. The 10-draw nulls cannot support significance claims. The tuned nulls were
  not run.
- Labels are TRRUST; "candidate non-edges" are undocumented, not confirmed absent.

SURPRISES
- The static (context-free) token embedding matches the residual-stream bilinear signal in lung.
- Fitting the true-label column jointly with the 100 rewired columns can change the in-fold alpha choice.
  2 of 171 M1 null rows differ from the standalone true-label run by about 0.06 AUROC on one fold
  (median difference 8e-5; null_results.csv, max_abs_diff_vs_true_run). Null comparisons use the jointly
  fitted column, so they stay internally consistent.
- The target-only control is far from 0.5 (0.54-0.63) even though non-edges are matched on target
  in-degree and detection deciles.
