INFERENCE
- On this benchmark the standard expression-based GRN baselines are as weak as every other scorer: within-TF AUROC
  0.51-0.54 in the three large contexts and 0.55-0.61 in the small ones, with no detectable gain over |co-expression|
  and no detectable difference from scGPT cosine. scGPT's H3 result therefore is not "below what an expression GRN method
  recovers"; equally, the data give no evidence that scGPT encodes more edge information than co-expression or GRN methods.
- The direction test is null: 19 of 20 ensemble tests have p > .13; the one exception (kidney_B GENIE3 T2, p = .0095) does
  not survive any multiplicity correction over 20 tests (Bonferroni .0025), its source-regulator cluster CI includes .5,
  the same method on T1 (5 seeds) gives .523 (p .24), and kidney_B shares cells with kidney_A
  (errata item 2). Tree-importance asymmetry does not recover TRRUST direction here.
- The within-TF permutation p-values are small (often at the 5e-4 floor) even where the two-way bootstrap CI includes 0.5
  (e.g. immune |coexpr| T1 .559 [.479,.652], p = 5e-4). The permutation null holds targets fixed and so ignores
  target-level dependence; the two-way bootstrap is the appropriate uncertainty statement and the paper should not quote
  these p-values as evidence of edge signal.

HYPOTHESES (not tested here)
- The ceiling may be set by the labels: TRRUST positives versus candidate non-edges matched on target in-degree contain
  many context-inactive or indirect edges, which no expression-derived scorer can separate in 256-3,000 cells.
- The higher AUROCs in immune / kidney_A for every scorer may reflect fewer, better-studied TFs rather than easier biology.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW / LIMITATIONS
- Not a test of arboreto's exact GENIE3/GRNBoost2 code (sklearn re-implementations with the specified hyperparameters).
- Only 2 T2 seeds: the T2 seed SD is an n=2 estimate. Seed variance is in any case small relative to sampling variance.
- immune (36 TFs, 547 positives) and kidney_A (29 TFs, 213 positives) have wide CIs; the direction test there has 62 and
  21 pairs and is uninformative. kidney_A and kidney_B are overlapping cells of one donor, not independent replicates.
- The direction test uses only TF-TF pairs documented in one direction; absence of a reverse edge in TRRUST/DoRothEA is
  not evidence that the reverse edge is absent.
- No conclusion about causal/mechanistic encoding in scGPT follows; all scorers are compared on one label set.
- 20 target fits on T2 had constant expression (importance 0; listed in grn_runs.csv).

SURPRISES
- 12x more cells (T2) did not help GRN methods in lung/lung_external/kidney_B; |co-expression| gained most in
  lung_external (T1 .524 -> T2 .551), where it is the highest-scoring method point-wise.
- GRNBoost2 leaves up to a third of pairs at exactly 0; GENIE3 nearly none.
