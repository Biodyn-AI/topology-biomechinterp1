ANSWER
Yes: physical-partner co-location survives conditioning on compartment in all three contexts, at 116-120 of the 120
cells (every significant cell has positive r). How much survives depends on how strongly you condition:
 - compartment-aware nulls (permutation within the 8 classes; MS rewiring that keeps each edge's class pair): the
   8-class partition accounts for r = 0.056-0.083 at the E2 top cell of each context (0.048-0.094 over the E2 top-3
   cells), so 81-86% of the excess over plain MS remains at the top cell (77-87% over the top-3 cells; median over
   120 cells 96-97%);
 - linear residualisation on the 8-level class factor (7 dummies + intercept) + log n_obs: 59-63% of top-cell r
   remains (median over cells 85-87%);
 - linear residualisation on the 30 most frequent GO-CC terms + log n_obs (the pre-specified design): 40-47% of
   top-cell r remains (median 68-72%). This is the most aggressive design and it probably over-removes (inference,
   from a post hoc control: 30 GO-BP terms, shared by only 39-41% of edges, remove as much at the median cell).
In lung, L3 SV1 (also the compartment axis) has the largest stratified-permutation null mean (0.075) and the lowest
30-CC residual ratio (0.404) of the 13 cells with r > 0.3; under the class-pair MS null several strong cells lose
slightly more (L10 SV3, L11 SV3, L9 SV3: 75-76% of the MS excess retained vs 81% for L3 SV1). A cell with little
compartment content (lung L7 SV6, picked post hoc: r 0.311, stratified null mean 0.005) keeps 95-98% of its MS
excess under both class-aware nulls and 94% under class residualisation, but 30-CC residualisation still removes
23% (ratio 0.769; design R^2 0.08).

INFERENCE
- Compartment co-membership is a real source of partner similarity: the 8-class partition accounts for about
  0.05-0.09 of r on the axes most aligned with compartment, and close to nothing on the median cell. Most of H1's
  effect is not a by-product of this coarse compartment partition (finer compartment structure is conditioned
  only by the residualisation). Both class-aware nulls agree (81-86% of the excess remains at the top cells), and
  they agree with the within-class analysis: class-centred coordinates on edges whose two ends share a class still
  give r of about 0.30.
- Residualisation gives smaller retained fractions than the nulls. The two answer different questions. The null
  asks how much r class membership alone would create. The residual r is the correlation of what remains after
  removing everything the annotations linearly predict, so it also removes functional similarity encoded in the
  annotations. Partners share at least one of the 30 CC terms 89% of the time, and BP terms remove as much at the
  median cell. The 30-term figure (40-47% retained at the top cells) is therefore a conservative figure for
  compartment-independent co-location, not an estimate of it. It is not a strict lower bound: residualisation can
  also under-remove (incomplete and unpropagated annotation, non-linear compartment effects), and the BP terms
  are themselves partly compartment-correlated, so the over-removal is inferred, not shown.
- The detection threshold does not drive H1. Family significance, top-cell r of about 0.37-0.45 and the
  replication criterion (same sign in lung_external, interval excluding 0) all hold at n_obs >= 3, 5 and 10. The
  matched E2 top-direction r falls slightly with stricter thresholds in lung (0.435 -> 0.366 for L3 SV1) as edges
  are lost; its intervals overlap the full-V interval at every threshold (descriptive: nested gene sets, no test).

HYPOTHESIS (untested)
The remaining co-location reflects shared expression programmes or pathways that go beyond compartment, for
example signalling ligand-receptor pairs or nuclear complexes. Within-class class-centred r of about 0.3 is
consistent with this, but F1 does not identify what the programmes are.

NOT SHOWN / LIMITATIONS
- F1 does not show that the model encodes binding, or that the residual signal is free of other shared
  annotations (complexes, pathways). GO annotations partly derive from interaction evidence and from the same
  literature as STRING (unannotated genes have almost no edges, partly because many are not protein-coding), so
  conditioning on GO can over-remove.
- The classes are coarse, single-label and depend on the chosen priority order. Only direct annotations are used;
  terms are not propagated. Two of the 30 CC terms (GO:0005615, also the top class term, and GO:0062023) are
  obsolete in the current go-basic.obo, and two (GO:0016021, GO:0005887; formerly 'integral component of (plasma)
  membrane') are alt_ids that the obo merges into GO:0016020 / GO:0005886, which is why results.json labels them
  'membrane' / 'plasma membrane'. They enter the design as separate direct-annotation columns, which is correct;
  only their labels mislead.
- Residualisation is linear OLS. The stratified permutation preserves class but not degree (null sd 0.014 vs MS
  0.008). The class-pair chain has lower acceptance and higher autocorrelation, and like MS it starts from the
  observed graph; its ergodicity is not proven. In (d) each edge set's own MS chain does not keep edges within (or
  between) classes, so the raw within-class z-scores are against a class-blind null; the class-centred r is the
  informative within-class statistic.
- Every p-value is at its permutation floor, so read the z-scores and intervals. The intervals are conditional on
  fixed coordinates and residuals (SVD and regression not refitted per subsample) and assume exchangeable vertices.
- The retained-fraction CIs are from vertex subsampling of the ratio; the null-based fractions have no interval.
- kidney_B at n_obs >= 10: the E2 top direction drifts (|corr| 0.79), so the matched r there is approximate.
- The post hoc controls (BP, MF, random, multi-label compartment) were not pre-specified and carry no test.
- immune and kidney_A were not run (out of scope).

SURPRISES
- Raw r on within-class edges (0.48-0.50) is higher than on all edges: same-class partners also share the class
  mean. Class-centring brings it to about 0.30.
- Between-class partners remain strongly assortative (raw 0.33-0.38; class-centred about 0.20), likely because the classes split
  the extracellular/secretory side of the axis into several classes (hypothesis, untested).
- The 30-CC design explains only 20-26% of the top coordinate's variance but removes 53-60% of its r: the
  annotation-explained component is disproportionately the component that partners share (inference).
