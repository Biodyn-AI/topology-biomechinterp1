E8 - Word2Vec co-occurrence-only instrument on H1/H2/H3 (sensitivity; CONVENTION3 §5 "E8 Word2Vec"; no pass/fail)
CONVENTION3 sha256 3e3249d1d0a8ce1bc3cf64639fe233b016535456f82432a8bbfcacdd64a05c01 (matches FROZEN.txt; results.json hash_match = true)
Answers R2.1 (the word-embedding model from the Introduction is now used) and feeds the instrument comparison.

QUESTION
Does an instrument that sees only which genes share a cell (the scGPT token sets, order shuffled, no
expression values, no attention) already recover the three signals the scGPT residual stream carries?
If it does, those signals in scGPT need no transformer to explain them. If it does not, the transformer
(or its 33M-cell pretraining) adds something.

DESIGN (what was run)
- Corpora (e8_corpus.py): every cell of each context's extraction h5ad (lung 15,000; lung_external 9,000;
  kidney_B 8,000; immune 20,000; kidney_A 11,376 cells), tokenised with A2's rule (non-zero genes, top 512
  by expression). Check: per-gene token counts over the A2-reproduced extraction cells equal
  layer_gene_embedding_count.npy exactly in all 5 contexts (corpus/<ctx>_meta.json).
- gensim 4.4.0 Word2Vec, sg=1, vector_size=256, min_count=1, negative=10, workers=2, 10 epochs, 5 seeds;
  token order and cell order re-shuffled on every pass. "full" = every cell; "extr" = the 192/256
  extraction cells only (lower bound). Every V gene is in every model's vocabulary (0 missing).
- Evaluation (e8_eval.py), on V (n = 1,972-5,648 genes), every representation mean-centred over V:
  H1a assortativity of SV1-10 (STRING-physical >= 700 edges within V), Maslov-Sneppen null N = 1,000, max-T
  over 10 SVs (over 120 SV x layer tests for scGPT); H1b basis-free pair-cosine AUROC vs one MS-rewired
  non-pair set, vertex subsampling m = n/2, B = 2,000; H2 detection-stratified LOO TF AUROC (Lambert),
  within-decile permutation N = 2,000; H3 within-TF stratified AUROC on h3_pairs, two-way cluster
  bootstrap B = 2,000. Paired deltas vs scGPT layers 0-11 on the same genes, pairs and resampling draws.
- Every vertex-subsampling interval (H1b) is given both ways: the pre-registered sqrt(m/n) interval (ci_lo/ci_hi)
  and the delete-d interval (*_deleted, half-widths x sqrt 2; CONVENTION3_ERRATA item 3). Below, H1b intervals are delete-d.
- H2 note: the LOO estimator is not centred at 0.5 under the null (Word2Vec null means 0.27-0.50, scGPT ~0.49),
  so raw AUROC < 0.5 does not mean anti-signal. W2V-vs-scGPT H2 deltas are quoted null-centred
  (delta_nullcentred*); raw deltas are in the CSV too.

DEVIATIONS / REDUCTIONS (stated exactly)
1. Window. The spec says window = 512. Measured cost: 1,912 s for 10 epochs on 256 lung cells, i.e. ~31 h per
   seed on the full lung corpus (x 5 seeds x 5 contexts). Not feasible on this machine. Production models use
   window = 5 (gensim default) with per-cell, per-pass shuffling, so each pass sees a random subset of each
   token's within-cell partners. The spec's alternative ("token order shuffled per cell per epoch") is met. The
   spec's window is met only in a calibration (extraction cells, seed 0, lung and immune):
   w512 x 10 epochs vs w5 x 10 epochs vs w5 with the pair-update count matched to w512 (571 / 365 epochs).
2. Training budget. Full-corpus w5 x 10-epoch models get about as many pair updates as the w512 calibration
   gets on 256 cells (lung 4.5e8 vs 4.5e8). That is ~1/58 of what the specified w512 full-corpus model would
   get. Saturation check added (e8_train_sat.py): seed 0, 40 epochs, lung and immune (saturation.csv).
3. Not reduced: N_MS = 1,000 and N_perm = 2,000 as the E8 spec says (the primary H1/H2 use more).
   assert_resolution passes for every significance claim (floor 1/1001 < 0.05/10; 1/2001 < 0.05).
4. gensim workers = 2 is not bit-reproducible; the seeds fix initialisation and the corpus stream only.
   gensim/Cython 3 prints "Exception ignored in our_dot_float" when a float dot product is exactly -1.0
   (the sentinel BLAS sdot uses for errors); that one dot product then counts as 0. About 50-85 occurrences per
   training log, against roughly 1e9 dot products per model. Benign.
5. Fixes to the previous agent's code: e8_merge.py read the macOS "._*.json" AppleDouble files on the exFAT
   drive and crashed (now excluded). It also lacked the delete-d H1b columns and the saturation table (both added).
   No other code changed. Adding the e40 models and re-running eval for lung and immune reproduced every earlier
   row exactly (max abs diff 0.0; reproduction_check.csv; earlier outputs kept in eval_v1/).

INFERENCE
- The H1-type signals (STRING neighbours lie close; SVs are assortative on STRING) do not need a transformer.
  A model that sees only within-cell co-occurrence of the same tokens yields STRING pair AUROCs of 0.60-0.74 and
  assortative SVs. scGPT is better by ~0.07-0.08 AUROC at its best (early) layers in 4/5 contexts. Its last
  layer is within 0.014-0.032 of co-occurrence; that small gap still excludes 0 in lung (borderline),
  lung_external and kidney_B. In immune, the two cannot be told apart at any layer.
- H2 (TF identity) splits by context. In the three larger-HVG contexts (lung, lung_external, kidney_B), co-occurrence
  alone gives a clear TF signal (0.60-0.66), and in lung, with 4x the training (seed 0, 40 epochs), it equals scGPT. In immune and
  kidney_A, scGPT's strong TF signal (up to 0.72 / 0.78) has no co-occurrence counterpart (W2V at null).
  That part of H2 is what co-occurrence over these cells cannot explain.
- H3 (edge relation): both instruments are weak (<= 0.66), and they cannot be told apart anywhere. This experiment
  gives no evidence that scGPT encodes TF-target relations beyond what co-occurrence gives. Equivalence is not
  claimed: the CIs are +/- 0.05-0.10.

HYPOTHESIS (not tested here)
- The immune / kidney_A H2 gap might reflect that those vocabularies are small (~2,000 genes) and that few
  detected genes are lost to truncation (A2: 99.6% / 96.5% of detected entries tokenised), so co-occurrence there is
  dominated by cell-type programmes rather than TF expression patterns.
  Or scGPT's TF signal there might come from pretraining across 33M cells, not from these cells.
  Neither is tested.
- The late-layer convergence of scGPT to co-occurrence-level H1b might mean that late layers keep mostly
  context-co-occurrence information. This is untested. CKA (0.2-0.6) shows the geometries are far from identical anyway.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW / LIMITATIONS
- It is not the specified window-512 full-corpus model. The full-corpus models are window 5 and get ~1/58 of the
  specified pair updates (lung). The saturation check covers 2 of 5 contexts and 1 seed. H1b, H3 and immune H2 had
  plateaued, but lung H2 was still rising (+0.034 from 10 to 40 epochs). So the 10-epoch H2 deltas in lung,
  lung_external and kidney_B may overstate scGPT's advantage. Only lung was checked.
- The comparison is not data-matched. W2V-full sees 8,000-20,000 cells of the context. scGPT's embeddings come from
  192/256 cells, but its weights come from ~33M pretraining cells. The extraction-cells-only W2V is the other
  bound, and it depends strongly on training budget (calibration).
- Single-seed calibration and saturation. Seed SD is small (<= 0.008 for full-corpus models, <= 0.016 for extraction-only).
- All intervals condition on the representation; seed variation is reported separately (sd_seeds), not
  propagated into the paired CIs.
- H1a uses N = 1,000 MS draws, so the max-T p floor is 0.001. The SV comparison is not index-matched.
- kidney_A and kidney_B share one donor's cells (errata item 2). They are not independent replications.
- Surprises: (i) the extraction-only w5 x 10-epoch models are badly under-trained, so the "lower bound" must be read
  from the matched-update models; (ii) in immune, co-occurrence H1b cannot be distinguished from scGPT at any layer (delete-d);
  (iii) the size of the LOO-null shift for Word2Vec in kidney_A (null mean 0.27 for the extraction models).
