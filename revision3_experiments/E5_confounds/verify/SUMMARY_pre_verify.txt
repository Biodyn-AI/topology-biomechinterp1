QUESTION
Does the within-TF scGPT edge signal (signed cosine of V-centred full-dimensional embeddings; H3 pairs = TRRUST positives plus
matched TRRUST-negative candidate pairs) survive adjustment for pair- and node-level nuisance covariates: log|co-expression|,
log mean expression, log n_obs, genome-wide STRING-physical (>=700) degree and GO annotation count of TF and target?

METHOD (as specified; deviations listed under LIMITATIONS)
- Conditional logistic regression, stratum = TF, per context x layer: y ~ z(score) + 5 target/pair covariates (z-scored).
  The 4 TF-level covariates are constant within a stratum, so they cancel from the conditional likelihood: the stratification
  adjusts for them and they cannot be estimated. TRRUST target in-degree not used (label leakage; non-edges are matched on it).
- statsmodels ConditionalLogit overflows / exceeds the recursion depth in the large strata (lung, lung_external, kidney_B have
  TFs with up to 367 positives among ~1,600 candidates). The same exact conditional likelihood is evaluated with a
  binomially normalised recursion with exact gradient/Hessian (e5_lib.py). Validated against statsmodels on 14 fits
  (validation_statsmodels.csv): identical log-likelihood at the same beta (max diff 2.3e-12); statsmodels' own score is 5e-14
  at the engine's beta; the engine's maximum is >= statsmodels' in every fit.
- Two-way (TF x target) pigeonhole cluster bootstrap, B = 500, refit each time, the same draws for every layer; LRT of the
  score term (chi2, 1 df); unadjusted conditional OR for comparison; joint within-TF label permutation, N = 500, one
  permutation for all 12 layers, statistic = mean over layers of the adjusted score coefficient (assert_resolution passed,
  floor 1/501 = 0.002, also at alpha/5).
- AUROC scale: score residualised on the covariates by OLS after within-TF centring, within-TF stratified AUROC of the
  residual with the same two-way bootstrap draws; r_rb = 2*AUROC - 1.
- Sensitivity: degree and GO count as log1p (point estimate + LRT).
- Continuity (kidney_A): round-2 run_confounds_sv57.py re-run verbatim (variant A, zero-row SVD), then with only the SVD
  changed to the observed-gene convention (B identity bands; B mapped bands via E9_screen/legacy_band_mapping.json), then the
  paper benchmark end to end (C: H3 pairs, E5 covariates, within-TF residual AUROC, two-way bootstrap).

INFERENCE
- The covariates named by the reviewers (co-expression, expression level, detection, PPI degree, annotation depth) do not
  explain the within-TF scGPT edge signal: adjustment removes 2-11% of the log-OR and <= 0.022 of AUROC. The edge signal in
  the three large contexts is small (residual AUROC 0.51-0.55) but not a by-product of these covariates.
- In lung / lung_external / kidney_B the residual AUROC's upper bound stays below the 0.60 smallest effect of interest at every
  layer, so adjustment does not change the "small" reading of H3 there (descriptive; B = 500 here, the pre-registered
  decision uses B = 2,000 in E4a). immune and kidney_A are too small (29-36 TFs) to bound the effect below 0.60.
- Continuity: what changes between the submitted analysis and the paper convention is mainly the evaluation design, not the
  covariates. Pooled random negatives with pair-level permutation make the SV2-SV7 band look significant in most layers; under
  within-TF stratification with two-way cluster intervals no SV band is distinguishable from 0 in kidney_A, while the full
  representation is (8/12 layers). The submitted SV5-SV7 null stays null under every convention.

SURPRISES
- Inference scale (inference_scale.csv): the two-way bootstrap SD of the adjusted coefficient is 1.8-3.1x the model-based SE
  and 1.8-3.0x the SD of the within-TF permutation null. Pair-level SEs and the within-TF label permutation treat candidate
  pairs as exchangeable within a TF and ignore that targets recur across TFs; their p-values (LRT p ~ 1e-14, permutation
  z 6-12) overstate precision. On the bootstrap scale the per-layer z = beta/SD is 3.5-5.4 (lung), 3.4-4.4 (lung_external),
  3.4-4.6 (kidney_B), 2.5-4.0 (immune), 1.8-3.7 (kidney_A). The bootstrap CIs are the intervals to report. Hypothesis (not
  tested here): the pre-registered within-TF permutation null for the H3 AUROC has the same problem (E4a lung: null SD 0.0058
  vs bootstrap SD 0.024).
- OR versus AUROC precision: in lung the OR CI excludes 1 at every layer while the raw AUROC CI includes 0.5 at 11/12 layers
  (bootstrap z of the AUROC 0.8-1.8). Bootstrap draws of the two statistics are correlated at 0.69-0.82, so they measure the
  same signal; the difference is weighting (stratum_weights.csv). The pooled within-TF AUROC weights a TF by n_pos x n_neg:
  the top 5 TFs carry 51% of the weight in lung (effective number of TFs 15). The conditional logit weights roughly by
  k(n-k)/n: top 5 carry 19% (effective number 76). The pooled AUROC is therefore dominated by a few heavily documented TFs,
  where the signal is weaker (E4a's macro AUROC 0.58 is above its pooled 0.54 in lung).

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW
- It does not show that the score encodes regulation. Only the listed covariates are adjusted for. Not adjusted: shared
  annotation between TF and target (e.g. GO-term or pathway overlap, as opposed to counts), genomic proximity, literature
  co-mention, and any confounder not in the specification.
- TF-level confounding is removed by design (stratification); the TF-level covariate effects are not estimated.
- No direction claim: cosine and |co-expression| are symmetric.
- The joint permutation p = 0.002 sits at the resolution floor and is anti-conservative under target clustering (above); it
  is not evidence of a large effect.
- immune and kidney_A are small (36 / 29 TFs, 547 / 213 positives) and kidney_B shares cells with kidney_A (errata item 2),
  so the five contexts are not five independent replications.

LIMITATIONS AND IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Model engine: own implementation of the exact conditional likelihood (statsmodels cannot fit the large strata); validated
  as above. Bootstrap/permutation refits used full Newton for immune, kidney_A, lung L0-L1, lung_external L0-L1, kidney_B
  L0-L3. All other layers used chord Newton (fixed Hessian), which reaches the same maximiser (|beta diff| <= 2.5e-8,
  check_fast.json). Point estimates and SEs are always full Newton. All 60 x (1,000 bootstrap + 500 permutation) refits
  converged.
- Co-expression = Pearson r on the extraction cells (expr_extraction.npy) entered as log(|r| + 1e-3); the floor is arbitrary.
- The bootstrap target multiplicity is implemented by replicating rows; the percentile pigeonhole bootstrap tends to
  overestimate variance, so the CIs may be somewhat conservative.
- The residualisation removes covariate-explained variance from the score only, not from the label (a semi-partial
  measure); the conditional-logit OR is the fully adjusted estimate.
- B = 500 / N = 500 as the E5 specification states (lower than the CONVENTION3 confirmatory B = 2,000 / N = 10,000); nothing
  was reduced relative to the E5 specification. Errata item 3 (delete-d scaling) does not apply: E5 uses no vertex
  subsampling.
- Variant A could not be reproduced exactly (unrecorded PYTHONHASHSEED in round 2); the 21-seed spread is reported instead.
- Resumption: the first agent's background jobs had finished all 60 context x layer checkpoints and the continuity runs
  before this session; nothing was recomputed. This session re-ran e5_merge.py and added e5_summarize.py (diagnostics only,
  no new fits). No errors were found in the earlier code. One earlier crash, in the reproduction-check sort of
