1. WHY THE SUBMITTED COMPARISON HAD 186 GENES (attrition.csv) -- direct evidence
- Vocabularies are large: 19,264-60,694 genes.
- Intersection of the three submitted models (Geneformer V1-10M, V2-104M, ESM2-15B): 18,907 genes.
- Intersection of all 11 static vocabularies (Tier A): 17,693 genes.
- The vocabulary intersection was therefore never the bottleneck. The 186 came from the gene list:
  - The submitted code started from the 209 genes named in the round-1 kidney_A edge dataset (cycle1_edge_dataset.tsv). That is 1,152 TRRUST
    edge and non-edge rows, so the list includes genes that appear only in sampled non-edges.
  - Of these, 187 have a non-zero kidney_A embedding at L0 (22 were never observed).
  - Intersecting with the three vocabularies removes only NEAT1, a lncRNA with no protein, leaving 186.
  - Intersecting with all 11 vocabularies also removes 8 HLA genes absent from tGPT, leaving 178 ("legacy178").
- So the restriction came from the curated kidney_A edge set, not from the vocabularies. kidney_A is also the smallest context: only 1,972 of
  its 4,803 genes are observed, and only 180 genes in that observed set take part in TRRUST edges.
- The same tasks fit on much larger sets, with these gene counts:
  - Tier A: 17,693 genes. 1,587 Lambert TFs; 775 TRRUST TFs vs 1,978 target-only genes; 73,430 STRING-physical>=700 pairs; 7,593 TRRUST edges
    vs 70,882 matched candidate non-edges.

- Null: random 186-gene subsets of the 2,753 TRRUST-labelled Tier A genes, matched on size, TF:target-only ratio (64:122) and lung
  mean-expression decile. The statistic is the TRRUST TF/target-only AUROC, estimator (i).
- For all 11 static models, the legacy-set value lies at the 98.8th-100th percentile of the matched null.

- Interpretation (inference): the submitted 186-gene set is an "easy" set. It inflates every model's absolute AUROC by roughly 0.1 but changes

WHAT THIS MEANS (inference, clearly separated)
- The submitted cross-model claim about ordering (protein LM > Geneformer > scGPT on TF identity) survives on 17,693 genes and in every
  context's detection-controlled Tier B set. The absolute numbers in the submitted Table 1 are inflated by an easy, curated gene set.
  Two of the four model values there also depended on a subset-fitted SVD band.
- TF identity is encoded most strongly by protein-sequence representations, including 32 hand-built descriptors. This suggests that "is a TF"
  is largely a protein-family / sequence property (DNA-binding domains, composition). Recovering it from an scFM residual stream therefore
  says little about learned regulatory biology. This last statement is a hypothesis; E6 does not test mechanism.
- The task ranking reverses for regulatory edges. Protein LMs and sequence descriptors are at chance once TF identity is controlled within TF,
  whereas expression-trained models (scGPT contextual and static, Geneformer V2, AIDO) are modestly above chance (0.52-0.66). Edge-level
  signal, where present, is therefore expression-derived, not sequence-derived.
- PPI neighbourhood is recovered at similar levels (0.60-0.79) by most models. Here no model family dominates.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW / LIMITATIONS
- E6 is a sensitivity analysis. Its p-values are not part of the pre-registered H1-H4 families and carry no confirmatory weight.
- Edge permutation p-values (within-TF label permutation) ignore target-level dependence: 49 of the 104 full-estimator edge rows with p < 0.05
  have a two-way bootstrap CI that includes 0.5. The two-way bootstrap interval is the trustworthy statement.
- Detection stratification in Tier A is mostly nominal (70% of genes imputed to the median lung decile). Static models' detection deciles are a
  documented proxy: lung n_obs where available, else the median.
- AIDO.Cell and scFoundation vectors are forward-pass outputs on one neutral cell, not learned token tables, so they are not like-for-like with
  the other static models.
- The scGPT contextual embeddings come from one extraction per context (192-256 cells). Deltas against contextual layers are conditional on that
  extraction.
- Tier B differences between layers and the "best layer" are selected post hoc. Ranges over all 12 layers are reported for this reason.
- The selection-bias null matches on size, TF ratio and lung expression decile (plus degree in one variant). It cannot rule out other
  properties of the curated set, such as annotation depth. It shows that the set is unrepresentative, not why.
- Identity labels (Lambert, TRRUST) and STRING carry known study bias. Better-studied genes may be easier for every model.
- pairwise_deltas.csv contains node-task deltas for all static model pairs and vs every contextual layer; pairwise_deltas_vs_scgpt.csv contains
  all tasks vs scGPT (static; contextual each layer; kidney_A L11 for the legacy sets). Contextual-vs-contextual layer deltas were not computed.
